# Product Principles

### Principle 1
Google Family Link remains the source of truth. Never duplicate parental controls. Only visualize.

### Principle 2
Children should understand. Parents should configure.

### Principle 3
Everything should reduce arguments. If a feature doesn't make family life easier, don't build it.

### Principle 4
Transparency over surveillance. The child should know exactly which information is collected and never feel watched.

### Principle 5
Fast. Opening the app should never be required to answer: "How much time do I have left?"

### Principle 6
Open by design. Every family owns its data.

### Principle 7
One codebase. Hosted and self-hosted users receive exactly the same features.
